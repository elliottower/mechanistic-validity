
## Cross-field supplement → standalone paper

`paper/supplement_cross_field.tex` was split out of the main paper on 2026-08-13.
It carries the eight disciplinary foundations, the domain routes for fields that
cannot intervene, and the evidential-capacity survey across twelve fields —
appendices B, C and D of the 82-page draft.

It is deliberately **standalone**: the main paper does not reference it, and it
does not require the main paper. Both build independently.
