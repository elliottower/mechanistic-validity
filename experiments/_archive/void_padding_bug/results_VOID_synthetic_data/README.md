# Void — computed on synthetic prompts

Every file here was produced before the experiments loaded published data. The IOI and
greater-than prompts were hand-written generators, and the resample pool was word salad
drawn from a fixed vocabulary. The numbers are not comparable to anything published and
none of them is reported anywhere.

Kept rather than deleted so the record shows what was run and discarded. Do not cite,
do not reason from, do not reuse.

E2a is the exception in kind but not in status: its corpus was real Pile text, so its
result may survive re-running, but it is re-run rather than carried over.
